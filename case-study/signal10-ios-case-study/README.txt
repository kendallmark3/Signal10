Repogenic case study: Signal10 for iPhone
=========================================

Contents
  index.html   The case study page. Self-contained: all styles are inline;
               the only external request is the Plus Jakarta Sans font from
               Google Fonts (the same font repogenic.com uses).
  images/      Seven app screenshots (JPEG, 900px wide) and the app icon.

To publish
  Upload the folder as-is, for example to repogenic.com/case-studies/signal10-ios/
  The page links to its images with relative paths (images/...), so keep the
  folder structure. Link to it from the site's Resources or Offerings page.

To fit it into the site's own template instead
  Copy everything between <main> and </main> into the template, copy the
  <style> block into the site's stylesheet, and keep the images folder next
  to the page.

Things to check before publishing
  - The "Talk to Repogenic" button points to https://www.repogenic.com/#contact
  - The closing paragraph states "App Store submission in about five days" as
    Repogenic's typical timeline. Edit it if that is not the claim you want.
